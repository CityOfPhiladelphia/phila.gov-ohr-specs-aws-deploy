FIRE SERVICE PARAMEDIC APPRENTICE     

                                                                                                                       

 

GENERAL DEFINITION

 

            This is training level apprentice emergency medical services work as part of the Fire Department’s state-registered Paramedic Apprenticeship Program. Employees observe and shadow full performance level emergency medical service (EMS) personnel in the provision of emergency medical care and patient transport. Employees participate in a structured, hybrid training program combining accredited paramedic education, supervised field experience, and formal mentorship, as well as on-the-job training under the direction of departmental instructors and field preceptors (PEERs). Employees learn to perform emergency medical services by observing EMS field personnel during medical emergencies, including patient assessment, treatment, and transport. Work involves moderate physical exertion, and potentially adverse weather conditions. Work is performed under the supervision of an EMS officer.  

 

 

ALLOCATING FACTORS:  (The following conditions must be met for a position to be allocated to this class.)

 

1.            The position must assist full performance level emergency service personnel in providing emergency medical services and patient transport.

2.            Positions must be located in the Fire Department.

 

TYPICAL EXAMPLES OF WORK (ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY)

 

            Learns to provide emergency medical care and patient assessment under supervision of emergency medical service personnel; observes and learns to perform pre-hospital interventions in accordance with established protocols; prepares and maintains ambulances, equipment, and medical supplies for emergency response.

            Observes and accompanies EMS personnel on emergency calls involving injury or illness; accompanies emergency medical service personnel in transporting patients to medical facilities; assists in lifting and moving patients; monitors patient progress and responds to evolving conditions; maintains confidentiality and compliance with HIPAA and patient rights; engages with PEER mentor in structured feedback and skill evaluation; participates in classroom, simulation and field-based training activities; observes and documents patient progress.    

            Learns to relay patient conditions during transfer of care to medical personnel.

            Learns to complete reports and documentation accurately as directed; maintains confidentiality of patient information; participates in required training activities and departmental drills.

            Performs related work as required.

           

REQUIRED KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITIES

 

KNOWLEDGE OF:

 

·                     computerized software applications

·                     the use of GPS-based navigation tools and route planning

·                     traffic laws and regulations in the State of Pennsylvania

·                     basic medical terminology and emergency care principles

 

 

ABILITY TO:

 

·                     learn to administer first aid and life support care in prehospital settings

·                     maintain composure and perform patient care duties under high-pressure conditions

·                     observe and learn techniques for patient assessment, stabilization, and transport

·                     understand and follow oral and written instructions

·                     communicate clearly and effectively

·                     learn to operate equipment within established safety guidelines

·                     engage effectively, with cultural awareness and professionalism, with EMS personnel, medical staff, and the public

·                     accept and apply constructive feedback in a learning environment

·                     lift, carry, and move patients and equipment

·                     keep accurate records and reports

·                     use a computer for the purpose of data entry, email, and the internet

·                     navigate training systems, simulation tools, and online learning platforms

 

 

Employees develop and are evaluated on competencies governed by PA Apprenticeship & Training Office standards and aligned with CAAHEP/NREMT certification.  Employees must complete required instruction hours.

 

MINIMUM ACCEPTABLE TRAINING AND EXPERIENCE (The following statement represents the minimum training and experience standards which will be used to admit or reject applicants for tests.  Applications submitted by candidates for this class will be reviewed based on training and experience requirements as approved on 7/26.)

 

EDUCATION:

 

            Completion of twelfth grade school or the equivalent and completion of the:

 

·         Randolph Technical High School EMT Program

 

OR

 

·         Philadelphia Fire Department Fire/EMS Explorers – EMS Readiness Track

 

AND

CERTIFICATION:

 

          Possession of a current Emergency Medical Technician (EMT) - Basic Certification issued by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.

 

ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENT:

           

Employees appointed on or after January 1, 2026, who do not pass the NREMT-P examination and obtain certification within the specified eighteen-month period will have their employment terminated without the right to appeal, grieve, litigate, or otherwise contest the termination.

 

PHYSICAL AND MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS

 

            Ability to physically perform the duties and to work in the environmental conditions required of a position in this class.

 

LICENSES, REGISTRATIONS, AND/OR CERTIFICATES

 

            Possession of a valid proper class motor vehicle operator’s license issued by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania within 6 months of appointment and during tenure of employment as a Fire Service Paramedic Apprentice.

 

AS REQUIRED BY THE PENNSYLVANIA EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES ACT RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR EMERGENCY VEHICLE OPERATORS:

 

            Employees in this class must have not been convicted of driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs within four years prior to the last date for filing applications and at any time during the tenure of employment in the class.

            Employees in this class must have not been convicted of reckless driving or had a driver’s license suspended due to use of drugs or alcohol or a moving traffic violation within two years prior to the last date for filing applications and at any time during tenure of employment.
