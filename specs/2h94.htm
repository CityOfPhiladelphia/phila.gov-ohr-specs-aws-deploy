LABOR PROTECTIONS SUPERVISOR

(Compliance)

(Engagement)

 

 

GENERAL DEFINITION

 

            This is supervisory work in the Department of Labor’s Office of Worker Protections in the area of specialization, directing staff engaged in compliance monitoring or labor engagement and outreach activities. Employees in this class supervise staff responsible for ensuring compliance with worker protections and ordinances, or for developing and implementing outreach and training initiatives to promote awareness of worker protections laws.  Employees assigned to the Compliance Specialty plan, assign, and review the work of staff engaged in investigating, monitoring, and enforcing Philadelphia’s worker protection ordinances.  Employees assigned to the Engagement Specialty plan, assign, and review the work of staff engaged in business and community outreach, stakeholder liaison, and educational programs to expand awareness of worker protections.  Work includes training staff, resolving complex or sensitive issues, and representing the department at meetings with employers, unions, City departments, and community organizations. Work is performed under the direction of an administrative superior.

 

 

ALLOCATING FACTORS:  (The following conditions must be met for a position to be allocated to this class.)

 

1.         Positions in this class must supervise a staff engaged in either compliance enforcement or labor engagement activities.

2.         Positions must be located in the Department of Labor.

 

TYPICAL EXAMPLES OF WORK (ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY)

 

ALL SPECIALTIES

           

            Develops program objectives for unit; plans, assigns, and reviews the work of subordinate staff; provides training, guidance, and performance feedback; ensures staff adhere to departmental policies, procedures, and applicable labor laws; conducts onboarding and training for newly hired staff.

            Prepares and reviews reports; maintains case data, outreach metrics, or compliance tracking systems; responds to escalated inquiries or complaints; resolves complex issues requiring interpretation of law or policy.

            Represents the department in meetings with contractors, unions, businesses, community organizations, and other stakeholders; assists in developing and implementing departmental strategy for compliance or engagement initiatives.

            Performs related work as required.

           

COMPLIANCE SPECIALTY

 

            Supervises staff engaged in responding to and investigating complaints related to paid sick leave, wage theft, wrongful discharge of parking employees, the Fair Workweek Ordinance, the Domestic Worker’s Bill of Rights, and other workplace protections. Directs staff in receiving and investigating inquiries and complaints; examining records to determine applicable labor laws; interviewing employees, managers, and business owners to gather facts; and conducting field visits or on-site investigations.

            Reviews the preparation of investigative findings, determinations, and reports; ensures consistent issuance of fines, violations, subpoenas, and notices as appropriate under the law; plans and executes on-site investigative activity; manages the maintenance of case files and records in Excel or other databases.

            Consults with leadership to inform compliance decisions and develop compliance policies; facilitates complaint-related conferences, mediation, or settlement meetings; supports outreach and communication efforts with stakeholders, including vulnerable and non- or limited-English speaking populations, the business community, and City Council.

 

ENGAGEMENT SPECIALTY

 

            Supervises staff engaged in business and community outreach to increase awareness of worker protections; plans and monitors the development of training programs and materials for employers, workers, community organizations, unions, and City departments.

            Plans and directs outreach and engagement campaigns in alignment with goals and priorities; directs staff in conducting presentations, trainings, and stakeholder events; collaborates with community leaders, business associations, and advocacy organizations to build support for departmental initiatives; assists in implementing communications strategies including social media, digital outreach, and multilingual education efforts; manages complaint intake and office email inbox management.

 

REQUIRED KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITIES

 

KNOWLEDGE OF:

 

·         engaging business, departmental and community organizations

·         current federal, state, and local laws and regulations, and court rulings related to labor laws involving worker protections

·         investigative methods and techniques

·         the problems encountered in establishing and conducting training and outreach and possible approaches to their solution for assignments in the Engagement specialty only

·         computer systems and software including Microsoft Office and commonly used applications

 

 

SKILL IN:

 

·         analyzing and interpreting labor law requirements

·         eliciting information

·         developing and delivering presentations and training

·         effectively communicating complex rules and procedures to staff and the public

·         presenting labor law material to a diverse group of business and community audiences

 

ABILITY TO:

 

·         supervise employees engaged in providing outreach and training in worker protection laws

·         assess training needs, evaluate performance, and deliver performance feedback to subordinate staff

·         effectively conduct classroom or on-the-job training programs related to labor laws

·         assist in developing training programs

·         maintain, analyze and interpret data regarding labor laws

·         establish and maintain effective working relationships with the business community, departmental personnel, other city agencies, diverse community groups, and the general public

·         present ideas effectively, both orally and in writing

·         disseminate information about labor laws

·         communicate and deliver presentations effectively to a broad range of diverse audiences

·         adapt messaging and utilize translation tools and communication platforms for individuals with varying levels of English proficiency

 

MINIMUM ACCEPTABLE TRAINING AND EXPERIENCE (The following statement represents the minimum training and experience standards which will be used to admit or reject applicants for tests.  Applications submitted by candidates for this class will be reviewed based on training and experience requirements as approved on 10/25.)

 

EDUCATION:

 

            Completion of a bachelor's degree program at an accredited college or university.

 

 

NOTE: Additional experience or a recognized, industry related certification may substitute for the education on a year for year basis.

 

AND

 

GENERAL EXPERIENCE:

 

             Two years of experience in investigations, labor relations, labor organizing, providing legal assistance, outreach or training related to legal rights or protections, paralegal support, or case management experience.

 

AND

 

COMPLIANCE SPECIALTY

 

SPECIFIC EXPERIENCE:

 

Option 1:

 

            Three years of experience as a Labor Compliance Investigator in the City’s Office of Worker Protections or equivalent state or federal labor-law agency.

 

OR

 

OPTION 2:

 

            One year of advanced-level experience as a Senior Labor Compliance Investigator in the City’s Office of Worker Protections or the equivalent conducting labor or employment-related investigations, including analyzing complaints, determining violations, and applying relevant wage, benefits, or workplace protection laws.

           

 

ENGAGEMENT SPECIALTY

 

SPECIFIC EXPERIENCE:

           

            Two years of experience as a Labor Engagement Specialist, or equivalent experience conducting outreach and training on labor and employee relations laws.

 

 

OR

 

            Any equivalent combination of education and experience determined to be acceptable by the Office of Human Resources.

 

PHYSICAL AND MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS

 

            Ability to physically perform the duties and to work in the environmental conditions required of a position in this class.

 

LICENSES, REGISTRATIONS AND/OR CERTIFICATES

 

            Possession of a valid proper class motor vehicle operator's license as issued by the Common­wealth of Pennsylvania prior to appointment and during tenure of employment, if required by work assignment.
