WASTE TRANSFER FACILITY SUPERVISOR

 

GENERAL DEFINITION

 

            This is supervisory work directing the operational movement of heavy equipment at the Streets Department’s Sanitation transfer facility.  The employee in this class serves as first assistant to a transfer plant superintendent.  Responsibility for planning, assigning and reviewing the work of a moderate number of operational employees and equipment operators on various shifts engaged in the operation of a transfer station is a significant aspect of the work.  Work involves some disagreeable environmental aspects in conducting inspectional tours of the tipping floor and loading areas and some moderate physical effort.  Work is performed under the general supervision of the Waste Transfer Facility Superintendent.

 

ALLOCATING FACTORS: (The following conditions must be met for a position to be approved for this class.)

1.    Only one position shall be allocated to the class in Sanitation.

 

TYPICAL EXAMPLES OF WORK (ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY)

 

            Plans, assigns and reviews the work of a crew of skilled semi-skilled, and unskilled employees engaged in refuse disposal activities; furnishes special instructions for the operation; reassigns personnel to various work projects to avoid backlog and to ensure the continuation of efficient refuse and recycling activities.

            Supervises tipping floor operations, by directing equipment operators in the safe and efficient movement of trash and recycling on the tipping floor areas and proper loading of transfer trailers; monitors loading areas to assure the trailers are not overweight.

            Inspects tipping floor and loading areas for damage and notifies the facility superintendent; conducts inspections of facility features, such as scale deck, to ensure proper operations; inspects access road areas and trailer parking areas; monitors scale data and compliance with radiation monitoring permit requirements; conducts initial radiation surveys as required; inspects the permitted area for the facility litter conditions and directs cleanup activities; assists the superintendent with compliance with the facility’s  operational plans as required by Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection (PADEP);  maintains compliance history documentation and records submitted to local, state or federal agencies; notifies higher level plant supervisor of inoperative equipment; recommends need for major repair and/or maintenance work

            Directs cleaning and maintenance operations on weekends; ensures that the plant and related equipment are in clean condition and properly operated so that air pollution or similar nuisances do not occur.

            Determines tipping area trash and recycling storage area levels to ascertain shift production and space available for the dumping of additional trash and recycling and compliance with PADEP permit requirements prepares requisitions and reports relating to plant operation; prepares reports of repair work accomplished during the shift; prepares and reviews employee time sheets.

            Performs related work as required.

 

 

REQUIRED KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITIES

 

KNOWLEDGE OF:

 

·         the occupational hazards and safety precautions in a variety of mechanical equipment used at transfer stations

·         the operation and maintenance of a top load transfer station

·         the operation and coordination of large and complex heavy-duty machinery

·         trash and recycling curbside collection operations

·         state and local requirements regarding environmental compliance and permit site requirements

·         Scale house operations

 

SKILL IN:

 

·         Preparation and maintaining work records and reports

·         Coordinating varies projects to meet timelines

 

ABILITY TO:

 

•         supervise employees engaged in operation at transfer stations

•         maintain records pertaining to facility operations

•         make estimates of time and materials

•         read and interpret blueprints and sketches

•         understand and follow oral and written instructions

•         establish and maintain effective working relationships with associates

 

 

MINIMUM ACCEPTABLE TRAINING AND EXPERIENCE (The following statement represents the minimum training and experience standards which will be used to admit or reject applicants for tests.  Applications submitted by candidates for this class will be reviewed based on training and experience requirements as approved on 9/25.)

 

 

EDUCATION:

 

            Education equivalent to completion of the tenth school grade.

 

AND

GENERAL EXPERIENCE:

 

            Two years of experience in the operation of mechanical equipment found in a transfer station or two years’ experience in heavy-duty equipment used in Streets operations.  

 

AND

SPECIFIC EXPERIENCE:

 

            Two years of experience as a Street Crew Chief 2 (7A41) engaged in sanitation, street, or highway repair activities or traffic sign work.

           

OR

            Any equivalent combination of education and experience determined to be acceptable by the Office of Human Resources.

 

PHYSICAL AND MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS

 

            Ability to physically perform the duties and to work in the environmental conditions required of a position in this class.

 

LICENSES, REGISTRATIONS AND/OR CERTIFICATES

 

            Possession of a valid proper class motor vehicle operator's license as issued by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania prior to appointment and during tenure of employment as a Waste Transfer Facility Supervisor if required by work assignment.
